# Investigated results and originality

The public catalog preserves the original automated assessment states. Independent comparisons subsequently classified the following three previously unresolved cases as routine consequences. Expert review is pending; this is a mathematical comparison, not a publication-priority certificate. No result in this snapshot is established as an original discovery.

* `model_116b5eaad944a7e29fed`: for connected finite simple graphs with order at least two and maximum degree at most two, the certified target is an Estrada lower bound `EE >= 4n/5 + 133m/90`. The domain consists of paths and cycles. Their exact classical adjacency spectra give a strictly stronger comparison at every order. See Martín and Estrada (2023), Equations33 and35, with the ordinary-exponential specialization in Equation18: [publisher primary paper](https://mdpi-res.com/d_attachment/mathematics/mathematics-11-01630/article_deploy/mathematics-11-01630.pdf). A proposed auxiliary lemma is still unproved and is not used as an assumption.
* `model_c71b19aef6e373b1bdc9`: for finite simple graphs with at least one edge, `2m - E >= (s - 2)^2/2`, where `s` is adjacency spectral spread. The published graph-energy lower bound by real adjacency rank, combined with the second spectral moment, yields a stronger bound with the additional nonnegative term `(rho - tau)^2/2`. Here `rho` and `-tau` are the largest and smallest adjacency eigenvalues. This argument applies to disconnected graphs and isolates as well. See [Akbari, Ghorbani and Zare](https://arxiv.org/abs/0709.3140).
* `hull3d_unique_neighbor_radius_ge_1_0_normalized_laplacian_spectral_gap_order_0_is_bipartite_f9946b253195`: the adjacency stopping radius is at least the normalized-Laplacian gap for bipartite graphs under the engine's full native conventions. Chung's noncomplete-graph gap bound at most one, the elementary exclusion of singleton stopping sets, the separate two-vertex case, and the zero-gap convention cover the complete guard. See [Chung's primary chapter](https://fanchung.ucsd.edu/research/cbms.pdf). This does not provide a growing stopping radius or a new decoding guarantee.
